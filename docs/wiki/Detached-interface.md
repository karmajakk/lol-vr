# Detached interface

While exploring, the screen only shows the **dungeon view**. The rest of the game interface is spread over windows you place wherever you like:

- the party (messages and portraits),
- the inventory,
- the spell scroll,
- the controls (arrows, options, rest),
- the compass.

At first they sit around the screen: the pointer moves from one to the other with no offset, so you can pick up an item on the floor and drop it into the inventory. If you aim just beside a window, the pointer snaps to the nearest one.

Each window is **independent**: moving or resizing the screen doesn't move the others, and each one is moved (brass bar) and resized (corners) on its own. In the library, they don't go through the floor or the walls (but they can still go through shelves and other objects).

During cutscenes, menus, the map and full-width scenes, the whole image comes back by itself. The ScummVM menu is shown as a whole image too.

A **character sheet** is shown in full on the screen, which widens to the right while it's open. The windows stay where they are; the compass window goes dark, because the sheet uses that part of the image.

**Classic screen** in the wrist menu (or the **Panels** button of the keyboard) shows the whole image all the time; **Reset layout** arranges the windows around the screen, wherever it is. To move or resize a window, see [Controls](Controls.md).
